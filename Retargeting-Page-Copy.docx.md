# **What this page is for**

This is the dedicated landing page for the retargeting audience — people who came to the site through either gateway (ADHD or Traders) and left without completing the diagnostic or buying. Per Manuj: the copy stays generic and does not name where they came from, because a single retargeting audience catches both doors. The tone is a reminder, not a fresh pitch. The page routes into the same diagnostic funnel (with the new readiness gate) and the same $47 action plan.

**Build notes**

* Give this its own URL, separate from the cold ADHD and Traders pages, so retargeting traffic is measurable on its own.

* Do NOT reference ADHD or trading anywhere. The visitor may have come from either.

* Destination after CTA: the diagnostic funnel with the Curious / Frustrated / Ready gate (see companion doc).

* Headlines are the first A/B test (per Manuj). Three variants are provided below — test these before touching anything else on the page.

# **How it fits the funnel**

Cold ads on the two gateways (ADHD and Traders) drive traffic and fill the retargeting pool. The Custom Audience (all visitors, exclude purchasers, 30 days) captures everyone who didn’t convert. This page is the third gateway — it reminds them and sends them back into the same diagnostic, now with the readiness gate that qualifies for genuine intent.
